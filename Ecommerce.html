Questions

What counts as a ticket?

One customer request or conversation in your helpdesk, whether it arrives by email, chat or message.

How do I find my ticket count?

Open your helpdesk reports and take the average monthly tickets over the last three months, excluding Black Friday and other peak months. If you’re unsure, choose “Not sure” on the form and we’ll work it out together.

Do you build the AI tool?

No custom code. We select, configure and tune existing AI support tools to an agreed target, and we measure the result.

What if my store is under 500 tickets a month?

Start with the $250 Fit & Cost Check. For smaller stores, the honest answer is often a low-cost tool and a simple setup, and we’ll tell you that.

What happens to my customer data?

We work only from exports you provide, store them securely, and delete them within 30 days of delivery. Please remove customer names and emails before sending.

Do you take commissions from vendors?

No. We take no commissions or referral fees from tool vendors.
